📊 🏭 Storage Watch

EU Storage at ~34% — Injection Season Underway but Pace Is Everything

EU gas storage at 34.3%, well below 5-year average. To hit 90% November mandate, Europe needs ~3,462 GWh/day. With Hormuz closed and TTF at €44, the economics are painful.

EU aggregate gas storage at approximately 34.3% full — significantly below the 5-year average for this time of year, per EnergyRiskIQ / AGSI+ data. At this fill rate, Europe must inject approximately 3,462 GWh/day on average to meet the EU-mandated 90% target by November 1. Maximum theoretical daily injection capacity: ~4,200 GWh/day — very little margin for disruptions.

Constraining factors: TTF at ~€44 makes injection expensive vs sub-€30 levels of 2023-24. EU Russian LNG spot ban in effect since April 25 removes ~9 bcm/yr. Hormuz closure strands ~20% of global LNG. Qatar's Ras Laffan under force majeure with full restoration unlikely before end-August.

💡 Trade Ideas

1. Long TTF Calendar — Buy Dec 2026 / Sell Jun 2026: Winter must price in refill risk. Buy Dec at €44.10, sell Jun at €44.21. Target spread widening to €5-10.

2. Long TTF Summer '26 Volatility: Any injection disruption spikes near-dated TTF. Summer implied vol likely underpriced. Buy Jun or Jul straddles.

3. Storage Arbitrage — Fixed-Price Injection Strip: Lock in TTF summer prices (~€43-44) for injection now. If TTF spikes in Q3, spread widens. Synthetic physical storage play via financial contracts.

🚢 LNG Flows

Golden Pass Ships First Cargo as US LNG Hits Overdrive — But Can It Replace Qatar?

Golden Pass LNG (9th US export terminal) ships first cargo. EIA expects record US LNG exports in 2026. But 20% of global LNG remains stranded by Hormuz — US growth can't bridge the gap.

Golden Pass LNG — the 9th US export terminal — has shipped its first cargo. The departure marks the formal entry of one of America's largest new LNG projects, per gCaptain / EIA. When fully complete, Golden Pass will have the third-highest nominal capacity behind Sabine Pass (3.6 Bcf/d) and Plaquemines (2.6 Bcf/d). Train 2 startup targeted for later 2026.

EIA projects record US LNG exports in 2026 with slightly higher utilization rates, driven by increased demand from Europe and Asia amid Hormuz disruptions, per EIA Today in Energy. Incremental capacity: 2-3 Bcf/d in 2026.

But Qatar gap persists: QatarEnergy says damage could take 5 years. Wood Mackenzie: full Ras Laffan restoration by end-August at earliest. Two trains permanently damaged. Hormuz adds $2-3/MMBtu to LNG freight costs (Cape of Good Hope routing).

JKM at $16.84/MMBtu (close 07 May), ▼-13.60% MoM but ▲+47.07% YoY. Decline reflects demand destruction, new US supply, and some Hormuz resolution expectation. JKM-TTF spread remains wide at ~$12-13/MMBtu equivalent.
